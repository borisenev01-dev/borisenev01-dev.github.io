Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026.

In short: MeshForge has no telemetry and never sends your projects to us. What you type to an AI goes only to the AI service you chose. We only see your email and order details when you buy.

Who we are

MeshForge is made by Boris Enev, based in the Netherlands. For anything about your data, email borisenev01@gmail.com.

The MeshForge app

What stays on your computer

Your projects, settings and chat history are stored only on your computer. API keys and your license key are encrypted with Windows' own data protection.

What the app sends, and to whom

RecipientWhatWhen and why
The AI service you choose (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, OpenRouter) or an AI app on your computer Your chat messages, a short text description of the scene (part names, sizes and positions), earlier messages in the conversation, the AI's tool results, and viewport images: when you tick “Include view”, and when the AI asks for a render of your model with its render_view tool Only when you send a message, so the AI can do what you asked. That service's own privacy policy applies, including whether it uses your content to train its models or lets people review it. Free tiers often do, so check before you send anything confidential. Local models (Ollama, LM Studio) keep everything on your computer.
Lemon Squeezy (Link, LLC), our license provider Your license key, your computer's name and an activation ID When you activate or deactivate a key, and about once a week to check it is still valid. This is needed to provide the license you bought.
GitHub (where updates are published) A request for the list of releases, including the app version. GitHub sees your IP address. At launch and every 6 hours, to find and download updates

The app has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising, and it doesn't send us anything.

Buying MeshForge

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record and processes your order, payment and billing details under its privacy policy. We receive your name, email address, country and order details, so we can send your license, help you and handle refunds. We keep order records for 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics. The Buy button takes you to Lemon Squeezy's checkout, which follows Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to answer it and keep it as long as needed for that, or for an order it relates to.

Data outside the EU

Lemon Squeezy and GitHub are based in the United States, as are most AI services, so your data can be processed there. These companies use safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Their privacy policies explain which they use.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and object to or restrict how we use it. Email us and we'll answer within a month. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority where you live.

We rely on these legal bases: performing our contract with you (licenses, updates, support), legal obligations (tax records), and our legitimate interest in keeping MeshForge secure and working.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
